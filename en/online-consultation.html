Online consultation on your documents

Send me the documents you have received: I review them and you get a written opinion, in Italian or English, on where things stand under Italian law and what is best to do. Fixed price, from anywhere.

Prices apply to a single matter and up to 20 pages of documents. For more complex matters or longer files you receive a personalised quote before paying. No VAT is charged (flat-rate tax scheme). An optional follow-up video call after the opinion can be arranged, at a price agreed in advance.

How it works

  1. Send your request with the form below: your contact details, the names of the other parties involved and a short description. Do not attach documents yet.
  2. Receive the confirmation within one working day, after a conflict of interest check, with the price, the terms and a link to pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, or by bank transfer.
  3. Send the documents to the address given in the confirmation.
  4. Receive the written opinion: an assessment of your situation, the risks, the deadlines and the options available, with an estimate of the cost of any further work.

What it does not include

  • drafting letters, claims or court documents, or attending hearings: if needed, you receive a separate quote;
  • defence in proceedings with deadlines of a few days: in that case call the office straight away.

If you then instruct me on the same matter, the cost of the consultation is deducted from the fee. The opinion concerns Italian law.
